School Library Management System

A comprehensive digital solution designed to streamline library operations — from cataloguing books and registering students to managing borrow records and generating insightful reports. Built to support librarians in maintaining an organized, efficient, and modern school library.

System Features

Book Catalogue

Easily add, update, and organize the library's entire book collection. Each entry captures the title, author, publisher, and publication details, giving librarians a clear and searchable catalogue at their fingertips.

Student Registry

Keep track of all enrolled students along with their academic details. The registry makes it easy to look up any student, confirm their identity, and view a summary of their borrowing activity.

Smart Borrow Tracking

Ensure accurate and reliable lending records by automatically preventing duplicate checkouts and validating return dates. Librarians can manage borrowing and returns with confidence, knowing the system enforces the rules in the background.

Report Generation

Quickly generate key library reports — including currently borrowed books, most popular titles, full borrow history, and a student summary. Each report can be exported as a PDF for record-keeping or sharing.

User Authentication

Keep the system secure with a dedicated login portal for authorized users. Supports account registration, password recovery, and a "remember me" option — ensuring only the right people have access to library operations.

Live Dashboard

Get an instant overview of the library's current status — total books, registered students, active borrows, and unreturned items — all displayed at a glance and refreshed in real time whenever data changes.
